# Pre-registration: minds-v7 (the deterministic Chief)
Rules are the defaults in `shared/engine/config.ts` (hashed into every run). Strict: fresh projection (<24h), 15+ games, top 6 by minutes and 24+ minutes, 2+ independent books quoting both sides, verified injury/identity/line/timestamp, a validated calibration for the statistic, no gap >= 25 points, matchup reason for gaps >= 15. Sam: conservative p >= 60% and EV >= 0. Val: conservative EV >= +3% and p >= 50%. Cat: market < 50%, calibrated model >= +10 points, conservative EV >= +2%, matchup reason. Caps: 1 per player and stat, 2 per player, 4 per game, 12 a day, Unders <= 65%. Leans are SHADOW_ONLY (raw EV >= +2%, raw p >= 50%, 1 per player, 8 a day). Lou parlays are RESEARCH_ONLY.
Evaluation: forward record only, strict tier, from the first v7 day; subgroup tables are exploratory. No profitability claim is made or implied.
