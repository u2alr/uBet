## What this means
- The 68 existing features already capture most of what these context features describe (rest days, back-to-backs, minutes trends, absent rotation minutes). Adding fatigue, travel, pressure, role and lineup-continuity measures did not produce a reliable accuracy gain.
- Travel, pressure (game-level closeness only, not clutch performance), role stability and lineup continuity show no consistent value. They stay out of every production path; the code (`research/context_features.py`) stays available behind no switch at all: nothing imports it except the research scripts.
- Not tested because no data exists in the repository: confirmed injury and starting-lineup reports, coaching changes, play-by-play pressure, anything psychological. None will be inferred.
- Nothing here involves a sportsbook price, so nothing here says anything about beating the market.
